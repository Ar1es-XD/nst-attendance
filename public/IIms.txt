# Newton School Attendance Tracker & Planner

> A production-grade, paper-like academic attendance dashboard, bunk capacity calculator, and what-if schedule simulator designed for Newton School of Technology (NST) undergraduate engineering students.

## System Overview
Newton School Attendance Tracker connects to the official Newton School Learning Management System (LMS) API hierarchy to extract live enrolled semesters, learning units (courses), and chronological lecture attendance ledger logs.

The platform eliminates guesswork by computing exact discrete mathematical thresholds for attendance compliance, protecting students from debarment while maximizing schedule flexibility.

## Mathematical Formulation & Causation Engine

The system computes two discrete integer invariants for any target threshold $T \in [0.5, 1.0]$:

1. **Bunkable Class Capacity ($B$)**:
   When current attendance $A$ meets or exceeds target threshold $T$ over total lectures $N$ ($A/N \ge T$):
   $$B = \left\lfloor \frac{A - T \cdot N}{T} \right\rfloor$$
   This represents the exact integer number of upcoming classes the student can skip consecutively without dropping below threshold $T$.

2. **Required Consecutive Recovery ($R$)**:
   When current attendance $A$ is below target threshold $T$ ($A/N < T$):
   $$R = \left\lceil \frac{T \cdot N - A}{1 - T} \right\rceil$$
   This represents the exact integer number of consecutive future lectures the student must attend to elevate their cumulative attendance rate back to $\ge T$.

3. **Causation Engine & Buffer Clamping**:
   - Compares aggregate vs. course-level standing to prevent debarment in isolated subjects even when aggregate rate is safe.
   - Evaluates next scheduled lecture against course-level buffers and generates immediate advisory flags.

## Core Features
- **Real-Time Academic Tracking**: Multi-semester hierarchy, live course health checks (Safe $\ge T$, Caution, Low Attendance).
- **Interactive What-If Stepper Simulator**: Increment or decrement attended/missed classes per course or batch simulate full days/weeks.
- **Verified Lecture Ledger**: Chronological log of all conducted and upcoming sessions with timestamps, lecture topics, whiteboard notes, and instructor profiles.
- **1-Click Slack DM Generator**: Respectful, humanized correspondence generator for faculty attendance inquiries, complete with honorific toggles (Sir/Ma'am) and tone temperature slider (0.0 Formal to 1.0 Expressive).
- **Custom Subject Buckets**: Group related courses (Labs, Theory, Electives) into aggregated buckets with custom thresholds.
- **Zero-Trust Privacy**: Zero external backend databases. Bearer tokens and local simulations are stored strictly inside the user's browser (`localStorage`) and never transmitted to third parties.
